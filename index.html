Let’s talk about your property ↗
Is there any obligation?

No. Asking for an offer does not commit you to a sale. Read any purchase agreement carefully before signing.

How do you work out the price?

We review nearby sales, the condition, repair needs, title, selling costs and investment risk. An as-is cash offer may be lower than a retail listing price. We will explain the offer so you can compare your options.

Do I need to make repairs?

No. We review the property as-is. Its condition will affect the offer, but you do not have to complete repairs before asking.

What if I have a mortgage, liens or owe more than the home is worth?

The closing professional checks balances and payoff requirements. A sale needs enough proceeds or separate lender and creditor agreements to address them. We cannot promise a short sale approval or debt forgiveness.

How fast can you close?

Tell us your preferred date. The actual timeline depends on title, property review, financing arrangements if any, and required approvals. A foreclosure deadline may limit what is possible.

Do I need to clean or empty the house?

Usually you can leave the property as-is. Tell us what you want to leave, and we will put any cleanout agreement in writing.

Can you buy a house in probate?

We can review it with the person authorized to act for the estate. The sale may depend on court approval, heir consent or other legal requirements. Your attorney can explain what applies.

Who pays closing costs?

Our written offer explains which standard closing costs we cover. Mortgage balances, taxes, liens and other seller obligations may still be paid from your proceeds. Review the closing statement before signing.

Are you a real estate agent?

We are a real estate investment company, not agents or brokers representing you. We may purchase directly or assign our purchase contract to another buyer. We will disclose the transaction structure in the agreement.

Can I do this from another state?

Yes, our conversations and document coordination can happen remotely. E-sign and remote closing options depend on the document, closing provider and state requirements.
